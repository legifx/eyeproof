How we cut payroll errors by 80%

Sam Ortiz · 6 min read · October 2026

Most payroll mistakes are not calculation errors. They come from data that changed after someone last looked at it: a new address, a contract amendment, a bonus agreed in a hallway.

We started treating every change as an event with an owner and a deadline. Nothing reaches a pay run without passing through that queue.

The fix was not more checks. It was fewer places where data can quietly go stale.

Within two quarters, corrections after a run dropped from 41 to 8 per thousand payslips.
